⚠ THE ONE MISSABLE TROPHY

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. One spot is on the balcony of Jason’s house in Chapter 1, after the mission “Down to Earth”, when Lucia invites a morning meditation. Do it before Chapter 1 ends — miss it and the trophy is gone for that playthrough (there is no mission replay). The other 17 spots can be done in free roam after the story.

Roadmap to the Platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

Difficulty 4/10 · ~200 hours · 1 playthrough · 0 online trophies · Free roam after the story · No mission replay · 15 manual save slots · Cheats disable trophies

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, don’t miss the balcony

    Finish Chapters 1–4 and the final mission (12 story trophies, incl. the Gold “You Gotta Know When to Get Off”). In Chapter 1, meditate on Jason’s balcony when Lucia asks — the only missable step in the whole list. Keep a manual save in a spare slot before each chapter ends.

    Yields: 12 story trophies + the missable meditation started.

  2. Stage 2 — Strangers, side jobs & the map

    Clear the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret — 8 strands for the Silver “Strangers & Freaks”), explore all 52 areas of Leonida, and start the big counters: WAiNK scans, card sets, vehicles, challenges, stunts.

    Yields: side trophies, “The Goodtime State”, early collectibles.

  3. Stage 3 — Robberies, grinds & 100%

    Bank vaults (5 of 8), gang operations (one per gang, 39 total), armored trucks, jewelry store, then the long counts for the Gold “A Long Road” (100%): 100 optional tasks, 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs, 8 bank vaults, 15 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 33 underwater discoveries, 100 of 125 Cocaine Flamingos, 42/42 Shooting Range gold, 42/42 drone time trials, 300 of 397 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, 15/15 Bret target decks, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals. Platinum pops last.

    Yields: Silver robbery trophies, Gold “A Long Road”, then Platinum.
